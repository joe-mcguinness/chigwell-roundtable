We're Chigwell Round Table, a group of men aged 18 to 45 who meet up, try new things and raise money for causes close to home.

Come and say hello What we're about

We meet on the 2nd and 4th Wednesday of every month.

What we do

Three things, done properly.

Round Table has been bringing young men together since 1927. In Chigwell it comes down to this.

01

Fellowship

A regular night out with a decent bunch of blokes. New friends locally, and a network of Tablers across the country and the world.

02

Fun

Socials, trips, challenges and the odd thing you'd never have tried on your own. If someone suggests it, we'll probably give it a go.

03

Community

Fundraising and hands-on help for local causes. Every pound we raise and every hour we give stays close to home.

Fancy coming along?

The easiest way to find out what we're up to is to follow us. Drop us a message or an email and we'll point you at the next meet.
